Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

IconVault ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information is collected when you use this site and why.

1. Information collected during payment

When you make a purchase or donation, our payment processor, Razorpay, may collect your name, email address, phone number, and payment details (card/UPI/bank) directly, in order to process the transaction securely. We do not receive or store your full card number, CVV, or UPI PIN — these are handled entirely by Razorpay under its own security and compliance standards.

2. What we do store

We may keep a record of your Payment ID, the item purchased, and the amount paid, for support and refund purposes only.

3. How information is used

Information collected is used only to: process your payment, deliver your purchased file, respond to support or refund requests, and comply with legal requirements.

4. Third parties

We share payment information only with Razorpay, our payment gateway partner, to the extent necessary to process your transaction. We do not sell your information to anyone.

5. Your rights

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about a transaction, or to request its deletion where legally possible.
