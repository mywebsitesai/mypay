Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

IconVault sells digital PNG icon files delivered instantly on successful payment, and also accepts optional voluntary donations. This policy explains when refunds apply.

1. Icon purchases

Because each icon is delivered to you immediately and automatically after payment, purchases are generally non-refundable once the download has started. However, we will issue a full refund if:

  • The payment was deducted but the file did not download due to a technical error on our end, or
  • The downloaded file is corrupted or does not open correctly.

To request this, email us at rushikeshkhode2601@gmail.com within 48 hours of purchase with your Payment ID (shown in your payment confirmation).

2. Donations

Donations made through the "Support this project" section are voluntary contributions and are non-refundable, except in case of a duplicate or accidental charge, which we will review on request.

3. Processing time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days and credited back to the original payment method used (card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet), as per your bank's own timelines.

4. Contact us

For any refund-related question, write to rushikeshkhode2601@gmail.com or call +91 7276324318.
